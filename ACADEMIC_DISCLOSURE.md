# Academic Disclosure

This repository presents a student research project in financial forecasting.

- The committed news/sentiment sample currently begins on **2026-04-22**.
- The current descriptive evidence is exploratory and does not establish statistical significance, causality, or a durable trading edge.
- Historical model evaluation uses chronological expanding-window splits.
- Model-performance statistics are reported only after the configured minimum OOS evaluation sample is available.
- A final model may be refit on all labelled observations for the next paper-trading prediction, but that refit is kept separate from the historical OOS evaluation.
- The project is for research and educational use only; no live-capital execution is included.
